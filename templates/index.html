Quick Safety Guide

4 Habits That Stop Email Scams

Check before you click or reply

Check the Real Address

  • Tap the sender name to see the full domain
  • Spot typos like @paypa1.com

Hover Over Links

  • Preview the URL before opening it
  • Avoid shortened or mismatched links

Pause on Panic

  • Ignore "24-hour account lock" threats
  • Verify via the official app or site

Protect OTPs & Files

  • Never share passwords or PINs by email
  • Skip unexpected .zip or .exe files
